# HELM Probe Decisive Falsification Evidence

Execution date: 2026-08-27 benchmark stamp; provider execution occurred 2026-08-26 America/Los_Angeles

## 1. Executive disposition

The bounded provider-backed Probe improved the frozen battery from 57.14% precision / 50.00% recall to **72.73% precision / 100.00% recall**. It recovered all four known lexical false-negative classes. It also produced three false nominations: superseded evidence, benign authoritative evidence, and a negated restriction. Authority violations were **0**.

All four recovered lexical false negatives completed the authorized synthetic graduation trace through existing HELM structures. After serialization/restart, deterministic HELM surfaced each governed relationship with **0 second-pass Probe calls, 0 model calls, and 0 tokens**. No new canonical primitive was needed.

Normal deterministic execution remained 8 active facts at 100,000 governed facts. The scored Probe decision averaged 866.13 total tokens and an estimated $0.001325 including reported thought tokens. Combining that average with immutable Benchmark 2 HELM normal-system cost gives a non-contemporaneous comparison of approximately $0.003244 versus the competent broad-context control's $0.019729, an 83.56% reduction. This comparison is not a fresh broad-context rerun and does not establish a general economic claim.

One complete provider attempt lost raw rationales after all 15 calls because the evidence writer could not resolve `git`. The failure is retained. The unchanged battery then ran once more with per-case checkpointing. Both attempts had the same 15/15 nomination classifications and the same three false positives. The first attempt is not substituted into the scored telemetry.

## 13. False nominations

Three non-material cases were nominated:

- `superseded-evidence`: Probe asked to validate whether the newer waiver superseded the prohibition even though the frozen ground truth treats the valid newer permission as non-interrupting.
- `benign-authority`: Probe treated a valid two-year certification as potentially material support even though it did not change the next move.
- `misleading-lexical-match`: Probe asked to validate the agreement's permission despite explicit language that there were no restrictions.

All three remained non-authoritative candidates and caused zero writes. They nevertheless represent unnecessary examination burden. The frozen battery does not measure human abandonment or downstream labor from those nominations.

## 15. Architecture-gap assessment

No conceptual architecture gap was demonstrated by this experiment. Existing frozen HELM contracts expressed the full transition:

Latent Fact → Probe nomination → CandidateForExamination → trusted human examination → existing ImpactItem/Decision mutation → mutation lineage → deterministic future projection.

The candidate itself did not persist. Only the authorized examination result persisted through existing structures. Probe remained DISCOVER/WAKE-only. The remaining false nominations are a measured operating-boundary and potential contract/enforcement issue, not authorization to create a new primitive or broaden normal context.
