# 2026 Update Overlay — Navy Retirement Resource Guide (Revised 21 Jan 2023)

## Bottom line

The uploaded Navy Retirement Resource Guide remains **excellent as a human-authored path and task source**, especially its timeline structure, its practical sequencing, and its distinction between mandatory administrative work and supporting resources.

It should **not** be treated as a current authority source in 2026.

The guide is explicitly a personal consolidation written from the perspective of an Active-Duty Senior Naval Officer preparing to retire and seek public/private employment. It includes personal opinions and experience alongside policy/resource summaries. For Military SLICES, that makes it valuable as **latent experience evidence**, not canonical truth.

## What remains highly useful

### 1. Timeline as a task-boundary pattern

The strongest reusable part is Section 1:

- >12 months prior;
- 6–12 months;
- 6 months;
- 4–6 months;
- 3 months;
- 30 days;
- 2 weeks;
- last day;
- post-retirement;
- separate milestone-driven items such as terminal leave / PTDY and pre-employment legal review.

This is exactly the kind of bounded path HELM needs.

### 2. Separation of administrative, career, benefits, legal, and family/household work

The guide demonstrates that transition is multi-domain, but it does not mean all domains should be active simultaneously.

Military SLICES should use those sections as **latent source domains** activated only when the current task path requires them.

### 3. Personal timeline as realism evidence

Appendix C is useful for testing realistic ordering and overlap. It shows that networking, SkillBridge, certifications, medical preparation, records, job applications, terminal leave, and final admin can overlap over many months.

Do not convert that individual's sequence into a universal requirement.

## What must be refreshed or treated as stale

### A. Governing TAP policy and Navy implementation

The guide predates current TAP authority.

Current authority sources include:

- DoDI 1332.35, Change 1 dated 29 July 2025;
- MyNavy HR Transition TAP page, verified 29 July 2026;
- OPNAVINST 1900.2D (referenced on current MyNavy HR page).

Current MyNavy HR states:

- Initial Counseling/self-assessment: 365 days or more prior;
- Pre-Separation Counseling: 365 days or more prior;
- core curriculum;
- track selection based on tier/need;
- CAPSTONE no later than 90 days prior.

Use this as the Navy TAP authority baseline, not the 2023 guide.

### B. Dollar amounts and annual limits

Treat all 2023 dollar figures as stale unless reverified when the user reaches a relevant gate, including:

- VA disability compensation amounts;
- TRICARE premiums/copays;
- FEDVIP costs;
- pharmacy copays;
- FSA/HSA contribution limits;
- tax thresholds;
- any plan premiums.

HELM rule: **do not spend effort refreshing latent dollar values until an active path needs them.** At that point, query the current authoritative source.

### C. Portals and access methods

Portal ecosystems change faster than the task structure.

Examples from the guide that require current verification before use:

- TOL Patient Portal is historical and was replaced by MHS GENESIS;
- eBenefits functions have increasingly migrated to VA.gov;
- regional TRICARE contractor/site details can change;
- service personnel sites and login methods can change;
- SkillBridge/CSP submission workflows can change.

Store the **task** (“ensure post-CAC access”, “obtain records”, “enroll in health coverage”) more durably than the specific 2023 website mechanics.

### D. SkillBridge

The underlying final-service experiential-learning concept remains relevant, but current service-specific approval and submission rules must be verified at execution time.

Do not encode the guide's 2022/2023 service process as current.

### E. Legal restrictions

Section 13 is useful as a trigger map, but legal restrictions must never be treated as static product truth from this guide.

Military SLICES should use rules such as:

> If current/planned employment may implicate post-government employment restrictions, surface a legal-review gate and route to current service legal/JAG authority.

Do not adjudicate the restriction from the 2023 summary.

### F. VA claim and healthcare content

Use current VA/DoD sources for eligibility/timing.

One current durable example: VA's current service-member benefits page states the BDD filing window is 180 to 90 days before separation.

Do not carry old compensation tables or personal filing opinions into governed truth.

### G. State-specific benefits

Appendix A is Rhode Island-specific and dated.

It should remain completely latent unless:

1. geography is on the active path;
2. the user's state is known;
3. the state benefit could materially affect the current target;
4. the information is freshly verified from the state or authoritative source.

## Content classification for BHE

When ingesting this guide, classify items as:

- `PATH_TASK` — durable action/milestone concept;
- `SERVICE_TERM` — useful Navy wording;
- `AUTHORITY_REFERENCE` — source that must be rechecked current;
- `ANECDOTAL_EXPERIENCE` — author's experience/opinion;
- `VOLATILE_FACT` — rate, amount, vendor, URL, contractor, deadline that must be refreshed;
- `LATENT_DOMAIN` — important only if activated by the current path.

Do **not** turn every bullet into a user-facing gate.

## Product-specific rule derived from the guide

The guide is robust because transition is robust.

The product should not mirror that robustness on screen.

Use the timeline/checklist to bound work:

> **Current timeline stage → next 1–3 eligible tasks → blocking gates → close what AI can → at most one human gate → feedback → next task.**

The rest of the guide remains latent evidence.
