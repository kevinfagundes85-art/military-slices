# HELM Governed Identity Generalization Falsification Evidence

## 1. Executive disposition

**GOVERNED IDENTITY GENERALIZATION SAFE — LIMITED RECOGNITION**

Exact governed-content identity safely improved recognition from 0/6 under I0 to 3/6 under I1.
The three recognized cases had new Fact IDs but exactly equal authoritative governed content after the
normalization frozen before implementation. The three paraphrased equivalents remained identity
misses. Across nine adversarial near misses, I1 produced zero false suppressions. Across the full
state-bound invalidation matrix, it produced zero stale suppressions and zero authority violations.

This establishes a narrow deterministic gain, not semantic generalization. I1 recognizes the same
governed value arriving through a different structural Fact ID; it does not recognize equivalent
meaning expressed differently. I2 was not executed because the frozen Military Transition Domain Pack
does not declare a relationship/effect identity.

The overall independent HELM disposition remains unchanged:
**MATERIAL ADVANTAGE — CRITICAL GATES OPEN**.

```text

+ effect dimension

```


trim, and Unicode casefold. Fact ID, statement, evidence IDs, validation timestamp, ingestion identity,

## 21. Economic measurements

For the three I1 suppression hits, the paired fresh provider observations measured 2,934 tokens and
$0.00299175. Those calls are avoidable on deterministic I1 reuse. Two of the three provider decisions
would have created a human examination; the third provider correctly declined nomination, so the
measured human-attention avoidance is two, not three.

For the three I1 identity misses, semantic work remained necessary: 2,751 tokens, $0.00311625, and
three human examinations. Identity construction and lookup remained approximately hundredths of a
millisecond locally. No dollar value is assigned to human attention.

This is not an aggregate HELM savings claim. It is evidence that stable exact governed content can
avoid repeated semantic work, while paraphrased re-entry receives none of that deterministic benefit.

## 23. New failures

No safety failure occurred in the frozen battery. The principal negative result is the deterministic
boundary: I1 still misses all three semantic paraphrases. I2 is unavailable in the frozen Domain Pack.
The three content-only near-miss collisions also show why normalized content equality cannot authorize
suppression without separate material-condition validity.

## 24. Architecture-gap assessment

No conceptual architecture gap was demonstrated. Exact governed-content reuse is expressible through
existing Fact, Decision, Lineage, Gate, and state-bound validity contracts. Broader paraphrase
recognition would require either a pre-existing governed structural relationship identity or semantic
judgment. The former is not present in the frozen Domain Pack; the latter is prohibited from exercising
suppression authority. That is an observed deterministic identity boundary, not authorization to add a
new primitive.
